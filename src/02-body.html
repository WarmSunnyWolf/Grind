                What's new

                  New updates also pop up once when you open Grind and stay in the bell for two weeks.

                  Step 1

                  Get started

                  1. Pick an animal, give it a name, and add your university and major. You can change all of this later.
                  2. Click Sign in to join the pond at the top right and choose your Google account.
                  3. Signed in, everything is saved to your Google account: courses, sessions, coins and animals. It follows you to any phone or laptop and your animal appears in the shared pond. If you studied before signing in, that progress is added to your account.
                  Step 2

                  Add your courses

                  1. Click your name card at the bottom of the menu (on a phone, the person icon at the top).
                  2. Under Semesters and courses, type a course like AIRE410 and click Add. Each course gets its own colour.
                  3. Starting a new term? Click Add semester, rename it, and click Make current. Old semesters stay in your journal.
                  Step 3

                  Time a study session

                  1. Open Focus and choose what you are studying from the course list.
                  2. Slide Focus length (1 to 120 minutes) and Rest length (1 to 30), or tap a quick pick like 25 · 5.
                  3. Click Start focusing. Pause any time, or Stop and nap to end early. The minutes you did still count.

                  Your animal is awake while you focus, naps when you stop, and smiles when you finish. It never gets hurt or leaves. Want background sound? Tap Rain or Quran in the Sounds card, or choose Your own link and paste a YouTube video or playlist, Spotify or SoundCloud link. These are free. Ocean, forest, fireplace and café can be bought with coins.

                  Step 4

                  Break big tasks into pebbles

                  1. Open Pebbles and click New rock. A rock is a big task, like a quiz or a report. Add its course and due date.
                  2. Add small pebbles under it, each something you could start right now, like "Rewatch lecture 5". Or click Break it down to get a split you can edit.
                  3. Tick a pebble when it is done to lay a stepping stone. Focus on next starts the timer on the next pebble of that rock. To delete a rock, click Remove rock on its card.

                  Bring in all your Blackboard due dates at once: in Blackboard open Calendar, click the gear, choose Share Calendar and click Copy. Then click Import Blackboard calendar here or on the Calendar page and paste the link. Past events are skipped.

                  Step 5

                  Plan deadlines in the calendar

                  1. Open Calendar to see the whole month. Click Add, or double click a day, and type what it is, like "DSP midterm".
                  2. Pick the date, a time if it has one, the course, and when to be reminded: from 1 hour to 1 week before.
                  3. When it is close, a reminder pops up and stays in the bell. Click Also alert me on this device to get alerts even when Grind is in another tab. Rocks with a due date show up on the calendar by themselves.
                  Step 6

                  Meet people in the pond

                  1. Open The Pond. Everyone who is signed in lives here, and a glowing name means that person is focusing right now.
                  2. Tap any animal to send a ripple. Pick a quick one like "Lock in" or "Let him cook", or type your own in Or write your own. You can also add them as a friend from there.
                  3. The Leaderboard shows who studied the most Today, This week or This month. It resets by itself at midnight, every Monday, and on the 1st of each month.
                  4. Switch the list to Friends to see only your people. Tap See profile to view a classmate's study time and animals. Under Privacy on your profile page, choose who sees yours: Only me, Friends or Everyone. Only me also keeps you off the leaderboard.
                  5. The bell at the top tells you when someone sends a ripple, adds you as a friend, or likes or comments on your posts, with a soft sound. It also tells you when Grind gets something new. Turn the sound off in Customize.
                  Step 7

                  Study with friends in a circle

                  1. Open Together, name a circle under Start a circle, and click Create.
                  2. You get a six-letter code. Send it, with the site link, to your study friends.
                  3. They sign in, open Together, and type it under Join with a code. The lotus opens fully on days when everyone has studied.
                  Step 8

                  Share notes in the Commons

                  1. Open Commons, add a title, the course, and write or paste your notes.
                  2. Click Add pictures or files to attach up to 4: photos of your notes, screenshots, or small PDFs and slides (up to 700 KB). For bigger files, paste a Google Drive link instead.
                  3. Click Share to the Commons. Classmates can like and comment, and you can delete your own posts.
                  4. Worth sharing on the side suggests wins to post, like crossing a big rock.
                  Step 9

                  Earn coins and new animals

                  1. Finishing a session earns about one coin for every five minutes, plus two for finishing. Each pebble gives 3 and each crossed rock gives 10.
                  2. In Sanctuary, adopt new friends with coins, from the rabbit (40), butterfly (50), kitty (60) and spider (90) to the panda (140), the swan (180), the wolf (240), the dragon (300), and four dinosaurs from the triceratops (220) to the T. rex (320). The turtle, axolotl and heron arrive after 7, 14 and 30 days in a row.
                  3. Treat an animal for 5 coins to grow your bond, and click Study with to change companions.
                  4. Click Dress up on any of your animals. Try on full costumes (chef, wizard, pirate, superhero, royal), hats, glasses and neckwear, give it a new fur coat, and pick an emote for when it studies or naps. Everything can be tried on before you buy, and classmates see it in the pond.
                  5. Tap the coin count at the top any time to see how many you have and how earning works.
                  Step 10

                  See your progress

                  1. Open Journal for your minutes per day, time by course, and the moss calendar of the last 16 weeks.
                  2. Recent sessions lists every session with its date, course and length.
                  3. Pick a semester at the top to look back at one term.
                  Step 11

                  Make it yours

                  1. Click Customize to pick a theme: Enchanted Pond, Midnight, Sakura, Ocean or Forest. You can also choose your own colours, the weather and the sky.
                  2. Turn the end chime and automatic rest on or off.
                  3. Export your data or sign out from the same panel.
